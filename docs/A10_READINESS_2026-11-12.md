Some patterns distinguish complete offices from descriptive modifiers: an
Assistant County Attorney can be a formal title, while former or interim can
describe the holder. The artifact does not settle every modifier's case.
Formal local names also retain some initials and suffixes; D-017's state-roster
primary-name preference does not authorize stripping them from every person.

The artifact is inconsistent on Fiscal Year, fiscal year and FY2027. Its Boca
rewrite additionally capitalizes Headquarters, although Peter's cited
correction retains lowercase headquarters. A Miami rewrite adds stacked
religious/academic honorifics. These do not justify new automatic rules.
Corporate suffixes remain in some rewrites, reinforcing the need to review
legal-entity distinctions instead of indiscriminately deleting them. Vote
reporting, omitted offices, source verification and name ordering are editorial
or reporting tasks, not mechanical fixes inferred from these examples.

## Pro tem and pro tempore

Pro tem is an established shortening of pro tempore, with the same meaning,
for the time being. It is not merely an informal substitute that must always
be expanded. The [New Jersey Legislature's official glossary](https://pub.njleg.gov/publications/Glossary_of_Terms.pdf)
records both forms. Cape Coral's [official election materials](https://www.capecoral.gov/departments/city_clerk/city_elections.php)
and [Ordinance 31-26](https://www.lee.vote/DocumentCenter/View/957/Amend-Ordinance-9-26-Reduce-Title-Ord-31-26-ENG?bidId=)
use Mayor Pro Tem as the office's label. These sources establish accepted
wording and specific office names, not Florida Politics capitalization law.

The artifact uses Mayor Pro Tem in Cape Coral and Fort Myers and Chair Pro Tem
in Riviera Beach. Recommended house treatment: capitalize the complete named
office, including a standalone reference to that office, while retaining its
verified form. Keep generic adverbial pro tem lowercase. Do not globally expand
pro tem, capitalize every occurrence, or substitute Vice Mayor or Vice Chair.
The phrases' shared meaning does not prove that differently constituted
offices are interchangeable. No new behavior is implemented by this review;
D-019 still covers only Speaker Pro Tempore and President Pro Tempore.

## Keep under local observation

- **New military abbreviation automation.** D-021's immediate-service and
  institution guards substantially improve safety, but the 40-body replay
  found only one retained automatic rank correction. Six incorrect prior
  edits were blocked. The 55-record family needs more naturally occurring
  military, civilian and named-program cases before release. Do not treat
  fixture count as proof of ordinary-use accuracy. Keep the abbreviation
  expansion and its new context findings together in the experimental set.
- **Inferred Police Department short references.** One earlier named agency
  does not prove that a later reference denotes that agency; stories can
  discuss unnamed neighboring agencies or general practices. More antecedent
  evidence is needed before releasing this inference.
- **Broad generic-title/body and Fiscal Year diagnostics.** Council,
  Commissioner, Deputy, Sergeant and fiscal-year forms can occur generically.
  Their findings do not silently edit text, but their volume and usefulness
  should be measured before enabling them broadly. Internal City of, legal
  intervener and adverse vote-tally findings also need real-copy precision
  review; do not promote them to fixes.
- **Additional offices and Pro Tem capitalization.** The scan supplies
  candidates, not a newly adopted inventory. Establish authority and scope,
  then add protected positive, negative and boundary cases.

Natural-use evaluation should record each proposed correction, its surrounding
context, whether an editor accepts it, and any missed institutional or
occupational reading. Reproduce every failure as a regression. The gate is
evidence across varied uses and no unresolved false edits, not a fixed number
of uneventful days. Never add source wording simply to satisfy a detector.

## Verification and release boundary

The full Windows Bash verifier now passes with portable Ruby 3.4.12, jq 1.8.2
and CI-pinned Vale 3.17.1, using Python 3.12: 238 Python tests, JSON/syntax and
whitespace checks, five Vale fixtures and four coverage manifests. Both the
bootstrap and cached-tool paths passed. The earlier 40-body replay and built
package smoke remain documented in the name-safety audit.

This recommendation has not yet been assembled into a release-only branch.
Before release, exclude the experimental behavior, reconcile tests and coverage
with the selected scope, update the version/changelog, run the full verifier
and installed-package smoke, and obtain green CI on the exact main commit.
Only an authorized release may create the separate immutable a10 tag and run
its tag CI. Consumer adoption remains separate.
