audit reports describe the full experimental branch; their counts and behavior
must not be mistaken for the contents of a10.

  complete titles; President/Vice President and the adopted compound/plural
  forms; City Manager, City Attorney, Council Member and Board Member with their
  admitted plural and -elect forms; Commissioner and Deputy before full names.

  Avenue/Boulevard/Street; rescope inflections and preconstruction.

  with no automatic expansion or deletion.

  preserve a name rather than inventing a new mapping or a person identity.

Existing quote permissions remain rule-specific. The new body-copy rules have
no new quotation access. Headline and presentation behavior are unchanged.
Findings are not replacement edits or instructions to reject valid copy.
